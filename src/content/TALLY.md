# Facet & question tally

A running record of what each rewritten entry is *set in* and what *shape* its
question takes, so the 328-entry pass doesn't quietly form new clusters.

> ⚠️ **`census-settings.mjs --unused` is no longer trustworthy as a "never used"
> list.** It matches literal keywords, so settings written in their own words
> rather than the taxonomy's word do not register. `beekeeping`, `glassblowing`,
> `quarry`, `sleeper train`, `angling`, `caving`, `pigeon loft` and others still
> report `0` despite being used in butter and later beans — the lines say "smoke
> goes into the hive", not "beekeeping". **The per-bean tables below are the real
> record of what has been used.** Treat `--unused` as a source of *candidate
> ideas*, never as proof a setting is free. Widening those regexes is an
> outstanding job.

**Rules being held to.** Five distinct settings inside one entry, never repeated
within it. Settings may recur across entries; the scene inside them may not.
Roughly one question in four is a standalone self-description rather than a
`You...` scene.

Question shapes: **S** = scene ending in `You...`, **Q** = standalone question.

### Structural note — the oscillation trap

Ten patch rounds, worst of the pass, and the cause is worth recording. Two
constraints pull against each other: **median ≥ 30** and **one facet ≤ 26**. With
only five facets, lengthening the middle of the distribution drags the shortest
facet up past 26, and trimming the shortest drops the median back below 30. I
went round that loop four times before spotting it.

The fix is to stop editing toward the checks one at a time and instead **place
all five lengths deliberately** — roughly 24 / 28 / 32 / 35 / 44 — which
satisfies every length rule at once. Worth doing at draft time for the remaining
beans rather than discovering it again in patch round six.

Also: my word-count estimates while drafting ran **2–4 words short every single
time**. Counting mechanically (a one-line node script over the batch JSON) and
editing to real numbers ended the grind immediately. Do that from the first lint.

Final: median 31, C-single 29%, A-scene-act 28%, 2 lint problems.

## Retro-fix: vague questions (39 entries, adzuki / black / butter / cannellini)

A pass over every question written before the concrete-question rule existed.
Audited all 120 in those four beans, left the 12 `# QUICK REVIEW` entries alone,
and rewrote **39** where no object or event was named — the reader was being
handed the shape of a dilemma with all the slots empty.

Typical of what changed:

| Was | Now |
| --- | --- |
| Something you took on has turned out far harder than you expected | You said you'd redo the bathroom in a fortnight and you're five weeks in with no working sink |
| You are waiting on something outside your control | The hospital said they'd ring with the results by six, and it's quarter past |
| Doing the right thing here will cost you something real | You've found out your best supplier uses labour he shouldn't, and dropping him loses you the contract |
| You are owed something and the other side has stopped responding | The garage has had four hundred pounds of yours for two months and has stopped answering the phone |
| Something happens that could be read as a sign | You find your grandmother's ring in a coat pocket on the morning you'd decided to sell her house |

Four needed their answers reworked too, because the answers had been written to
the abstraction rather than to a scene — `spicy-boiled-cannellini` ("take nine,
because six wasn't enough" only parses against an unnamed job),
`sweet-smoked-cannellini` (handling "an object, or a person" — the answers had to
pick one), `bitter-fermented-cannellini` and its replacement scene.

`bitter-fermented-cannellini` was replaced outright rather than anchored: its old
question was the *someone states a wrong fact and the table nods along* beat,
which QUESTIONS.md lists as retired with ~12 near-duplicates already in the bank.

Two checks worth repeating on any future retro-fix: the new scene must not
collide with one of that entry's own facets (caught `sweet-smoked-black`, whose
first draft reused the bird-at-the-window from its own `facetHigh`, and
`umami-smoked-black`, which reused its own two-minutes' silence), and the answer
grammar has to keep matching the question shape.

### Structural note — the rotation works as a rewrite, and only as a rewrite

Third batch running, and the first draft came back the most templated yet:
`facetMost` D:24 of 27, `facetHigh` C:24, `facetMid` C:25, `facetLow` C:24,

What did work, and is worth keeping: the rotation was applied **A-weighted** this
time — `SH = [A, C, A, D]` on a four-cycle rather than `[A, C, D]` on three — to
compensate for the linter reclassifying `A-scene-act` lines into its `F-late-you`,
`B-act-first` and `E-speech` buckets. That landed all three shapes under 30% at
the first attempt after the rewrite, where mung needed three extra rounds to get
there. Keep the four-cycle for pinto.

naming the object first. It needed a second pass of 26 opener rewrites. Watch the
opener histogram *after* the rotation rewrite, not just on the first lint.

## The loop, per bean

1. **Read the batch context** — trait/excess/inverse, the five fixed
   `facet*Title` puns, and any surviving `fortune*` lines (facets must not reuse
   a fortune's image; `seasonalFortune` is exempt).
2. **Assign five settings per entry before drafting**, from the thin end of
   `node scripts/census-settings.mjs --unused`. Distinct within an entry; may
   recur across entries, but never the same scene twice.
3. **Assign shapes and lengths before drafting too** — this is where both batches
   went wrong. Left to itself the prose collapses onto one skeleton (adzuki went
   56% `[Scene]. You [verb]`) or overcorrects onto another (black's first draft
   went 47% single-sentence).
4. **Draft to a batch JSON**, then `node scripts/lint-facets.mjs --file=<batch>`
   until the numbers look like the targets below.
5. **Apply** with `node scripts/apply-batch.mjs <batch>` — it refuses to
   overwrite populated fields unless given `--force`.
6. **Append the settings table here**, plus new settings and a watch list.

## Thresholds that were recalibrated (don't "fix" them back)

- Closing-verb cap is a **share** (8%), not an absolute count — 2 was meaningless
  across 135 facets.

  single-sentence lines that monotony reappeared from the other direction.
- Number words count as concrete openers, so "Ninety seconds of totality" passes.
- `castle` and `gamekeeper` in the settings census needed word boundaries —
  `king` was matching inside *looking*, `ranger` inside *stranger*.
