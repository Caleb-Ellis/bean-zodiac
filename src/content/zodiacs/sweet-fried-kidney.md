Caramelised Kidney Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most protective — what moves between them and the Beans they love is very little.

Kidney Beans stand at the front when difficulty arrives; Sweet Beans make the motivation genuine care; Fried Beans respond immediately, committed before the question finishes forming.

The Caramelised Kidney Bean's challenge is protection that leaves room for autonomy. They must learn to ask whether someone needs cover or needs to find their own way through, and tolerate the answer either way.
