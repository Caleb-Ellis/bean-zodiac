Cured Black Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most withering — their silences have a way of completing the sentence faster than any word could.

Black Beans weigh before speaking; Sour Beans reduce to what is precise and unsparing; Smoked Beans make the assessment arrive from an angle no one was covering.

The Cured Black Bean's challenge is mercy. A withering look from nowhere is very difficult to recover from. These Beans must occasionally make their standards legible before applying them.
