Sichuan Black Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most suppressed — the intensity is real and total, and none of it shows.

Black Beans hold everything below the surface; Spicy Beans make that intensity the whole condition; Dried Beans concentrate it and seal it in, with no natural outlet.

The Sichuan Black Bean's challenge is the cost of holding it down. What is suppressed does not resolve — it accumulates. These Beans must find a way to let some of it through before the effort of containment becomes more consuming than the thing being contained.
