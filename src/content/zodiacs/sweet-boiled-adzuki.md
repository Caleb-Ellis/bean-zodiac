Candied Adzuki Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most heartening. They turn up the evening your engagement is called off, and by eleven you have laughed for the first time in a month. Occasionally the laughing starts before the crying has finished. Agreeing that it's all hopeless is one comfort they can't give.
