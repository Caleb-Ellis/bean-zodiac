Glazed Adzuki Beans are the Bean Zodiac's happiest — and not in a way that needs to be explained or justified. The joy is just there, available, like a second batch already in the oven.

Adzuki Beans mark every occasion worth marking; Sweet Beans make everything an occasion worth marking; Roasted Beans make everyone glad they showed up. The Glazed Adzuki does not look for reasons to be happy. It finds them anyway.

The Glazed Adzuki's challenge is what lives underneath. Joy this effortless can become a habit of not looking down. The happiest Bean in the zodiac still has to check, occasionally, whether everything is actually fine.
