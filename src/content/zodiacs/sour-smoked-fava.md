Cured Fava Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most cautionary. Before you go up the ladder they tell you exactly how they came off theirs, and you hold on tighter. A passing headache can end with them reading you the symptoms of a stroke. Saying it'll probably be fine, with no "but" after it, is a trick they never learned.
