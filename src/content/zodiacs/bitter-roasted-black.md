Wood-Fired Black Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most elegant — depth that needs no announcement. They finish the work in silence and post only the finished bowl, once, and only when it's ready; their rooms hold one good chair and a lamp and a lot of breathing floor. At a crowded table they say little, and the room leans in anyway.

But the restraint that reads as taste up close reads as a closed door from across a room. The one-line reply lands cold to someone who wanted warmth; the depth held so carefully in reserve gets mistaken for a depth that isn't there. Refined a notch too far, elegance stops being an invitation and starts being a wall — and they rarely notice they've built it.
