Rendered Cannellini Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most confiding — they choose the ear carefully, and once chosen, they offer the held thing whole.

Cannellini Beans select what to share and with whom; Umami Beans give weight to what is shared; Roasted Beans make the offering feel like warmth received rather than weight transferred.

The Rendered Cannellini Bean's challenge is discernment. Not every room has earned the held thing, and learning which ear is the right one is the work of the season.
