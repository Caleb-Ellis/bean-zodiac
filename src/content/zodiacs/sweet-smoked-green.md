Barbecued Green Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most iridescent — they catch the light differently depending on where you are standing.

Green Beans are bright and urgent and best caught at exactly the right moment; Sweet Beans extend that brightness with an ease that feels natural; Smoked Beans give it a shifting quality that changes with the angle.

The Barbecued Green Bean's challenge is consistency. What shifts with every angle eventually becomes unreliable. These Beans must offer one reading that holds in any light.
