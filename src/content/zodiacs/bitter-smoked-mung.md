Charcoal Mung Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most anonymous. The flooded drain got cleared at four in the morning, and they let the neighbours thank the wrong person for a fortnight. Sometimes the person who owes them most has no idea who to ring. Putting their name at the bottom of it costs them more than the work did.
