Glazed Chickpeas are the Bean Zodiac's most fun — and unlike most things that know they're fun, they wear it lightly. The good time doesn't announce itself; it just happens, and everyone around them ends up having it.

Chickpeas go everywhere and fit everywhere; Sweet Beans lower the cost of admission; Roasted Beans fill any space with something irresistible. Together, they produce the one who makes a two-hour queue feel like the best part of the night.

The Glazed Chickpea's challenge is sincerity. Fun used as a habit can become a way of avoiding the harder things. The Glazed Chickpea's best quality requires them to occasionally set it down — and trust they can pick it up again.
