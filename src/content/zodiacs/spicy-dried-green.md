Sichuan Green Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most electric — the energy was already immediate; the heat has made it impossible to ignore.

Green Beans arrive fast and charged; Spicy Beans pour intensity on top; Dried Beans concentrate it until the smallest contact produces a current.

The Sichuan Green Bean's challenge is the room that can't keep up. Not every situation can absorb this much energy, and not every Bean wants to. These Beans must learn when the voltage is welcome — and when to dial it back.
