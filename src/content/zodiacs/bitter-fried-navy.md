Scorched Navy Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most severe. They wrote the rule, broke it once themselves, and took the same fortnight off the team anybody else would have. The apology gets accepted and the ban stands anyway. Letting it go once for somebody who would never do it again is not a call they will make.
