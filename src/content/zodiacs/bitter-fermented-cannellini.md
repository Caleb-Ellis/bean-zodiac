Cultured Cannellini Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most scholarly — they steep a long time in the dark and surface only when there is something worth setting on the table.

Cannellini refines what passes through it; Bitter beans favour what rewards sustained attention, the cicoria midrib chewed slowly rather than swallowed; Fermented beans do their work unwatched, the brine clouding as the days accumulate.

The Cultured Cannellini Bean's trouble is translation. A crock kept to oneself spoils into private knowledge. The work is to grate the rind, tear the leaves, and serve the broth while it is still hot enough to be of use to the room.
