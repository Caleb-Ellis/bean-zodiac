Peri-Peri Black Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most imposing. They take a seat at the back of the residents' meeting, say nothing, and the man shouting at the front begins to lose his thread. At some dinners nobody else manages a word, and they've barely spoken either. Slipping in unnoticed is a trick they've never pulled off.
