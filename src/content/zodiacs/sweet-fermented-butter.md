Funky Butter Beans are the Bean Zodiac's silliest — not performed, not deployed for effect, just naturally and cheerfully absurd in a way that makes everything around them feel less serious than it thought it was.

Butter Beans take nothing at high speed; Sweet Beans find the lighter side of most things; Fermented Beans arrive at unexpected conclusions by a process no one else can follow. The result is a Bean who sees the funny angle before anyone else and cannot help but take it.

The Funky Butter Bean's challenge is being taken seriously when it actually matters. The silliness is real, and so is everything beneath it — but other Beans sometimes forget the second part.
