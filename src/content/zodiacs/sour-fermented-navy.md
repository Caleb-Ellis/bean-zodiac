Pickled Navy Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most cynical — they have seen what is behind the curtain and cannot unsee it.

Navy Beans believe in systems because they see what happens when they fail; Sour Beans strip comfortable illusions; Fermented Beans convert hope into accuracy.

The Pickled Navy Bean's challenge is seeing something survive the scrutiny. They must hold cynicism lightly enough to be surprised.
