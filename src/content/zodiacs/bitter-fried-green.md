Scorched Green Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most excitable — they bring full enthusiasm with clear direction.

Green Bean moves fast and wants to; Fried means no interval between impulse and action; Bitter Beans provide direction, not dampening.

The Scorched Green Bean's challenge is the thing that isn't worth it. Most things pass Bitter's filter; a few don't, and they must trust that. Not every door deserves the full sprint.
