Scorched Chickpeas move on the opportunity in front of them. The pan is hot, the flour is in the cupboard, and that is enough — the fritter takes its shape in the oil, not on paper.

Chickpea brings the readiness of a pantry staple; Bitter sharpens the appetite that drives them; Fried commits the gesture before doubt has time to assemble.

Their work is to finish what they start. A kitchen full of half-mounted aiolis and resting batters feeds no one; the second pan should not go on until the first is plated.
