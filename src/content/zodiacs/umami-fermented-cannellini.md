Miso Cannellini Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most rarefied. One baker across the city does the only bread they count as bread, and it means a seven o'clock bus on a Saturday. Sometimes why it's better takes twenty minutes and words nobody else uses. Finding what everyone else is having perfectly nice is a knack they have lost.
