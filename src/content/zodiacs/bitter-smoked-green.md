Charcoal Green Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most eerie. They call back before the friend has said anything is wrong, pay the small debt before it's been asked for, wake at three to move the furniture and turn out to have been right about the room. Things tend to rearrange themselves around their having already been there.

What they can't do is say why. The urgency is real and usually correct, but it arrives with no return address, so the message sent at five in the morning lands as alarming rather than helpful. A hunch nobody can trace doesn't move people — it just unsettles them. Asked what they're running toward, they rarely have an answer, only the certainty that they should be running.
