Kidney Beans protect absolutely; Sour Beans clarify what is worth fighting for; Dried Beans remove everything that isn't the fight.

The Dehydrated Kidney Bean's challenge is what happens after the battle. These Beans know how to commit. They must also learn when the thing they fought for has already been won — or lost.
