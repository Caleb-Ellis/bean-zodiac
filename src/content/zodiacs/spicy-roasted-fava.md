Peri-Peri Fava Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most competitive. Two rounds into a friendly board game, they know who's winning, who's bluffing and how to stop both of them. Occasionally the friend they beat stops coming round for games. Everybody winning together sounds to them a lot like nobody winning.
