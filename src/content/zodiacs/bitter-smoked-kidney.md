Charcoal Kidney Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most haunted — driven by something whose source has become impossible to see.

Kidney Beans feel everything at full intensity; Bitter Beans sharpen the feeling until it could cut; Smoked Beans give it the quality of heat without a visible flame — present and real, origin unknown.

The Charcoal Kidney Bean's challenge is diagnosis. Haunted people aren't suffering for nothing — they just haven't found what they're still carrying. These Beans must trace the intensity back to what is actually there.
