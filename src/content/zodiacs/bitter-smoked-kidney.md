Charcoal Kidney Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most haunted. They feel everything at full volume, and the feeling lingers the way smoke clings to a kitchen long after the coals go cold. Their friends learn which names and which songs send them quiet for an hour, and route around them kindly, and the routing works.

The trouble is they mistake old heat for new. The flare in the chest is genuine but it's leftover from 2019, and they aim it at whoever's in the room — scorching the thing two rooms over from what they're actually looking at. There's one drawer they steer around every morning rather than open. It isn't that they're suffering for nothing; it's that they've stopped checking what they're still carrying.
