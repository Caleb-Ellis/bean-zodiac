Sichuan Cannellini Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most categorical. Asked whether the flat is worth the rent, they answer in one word, with no "it depends" on the end. Evidence to the contrary is occasionally informed that it's mistaken. Thinking one thing on Monday and the opposite by Thursday is a luxury they have never allowed themselves.
