Braised Kidney Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most stubborn — pressure doesn't move them.

Kidney Beans hold their shape under the longest cooking; Spicy Beans carry internal heat that makes the wait feel like insistence; Boiled Beans surround the core conviction while changing everything else.

The Braised Kidney Bean's challenge is knowing the difference between tenacity and refusal. They must learn to distinguish whether they hold on for principle or merely for obstinacy.
