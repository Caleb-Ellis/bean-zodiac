Candied Black Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most steadfast — steadiness through temperament, not discipline.

Black Beans hold their shape through pressure; Sweet Beans extend that reliability with warmth; Boiled Beans work the quality all the way through.

The Candied Black Bean's challenge is self-renewal. They must extend their steadfastness inward, with the same reliability they offer outward.
