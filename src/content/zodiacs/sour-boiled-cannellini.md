Brined Cannellini Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most exacting — they hold a standard consistently across all contexts and time.

Cannellini Beans are already particular about what they accompany; Sour Beans strip away sentiment; Boiled Beans refuse to rush.

The Brined Cannellini Bean's challenge is knowing when the standard serves the work. They must learn to distinguish between rigor and rigidity.
