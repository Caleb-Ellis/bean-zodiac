Charcoal Butter Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most adrift, and the calmest company you'll ever lose the thread of. Sitting with one feels like sitting near water; the temperature in the room drops a degree just from their being there. They notice the light, the long view, the thing nobody else has slowed down enough to see.

The drift, though, is real. Ask a direct question and there's a pause while they come back from wherever they'd gone — and sometimes they don't, quite, and the kettle screams while they finish a thought about the kettle. The person across the table waited a beat too long to be answered, and learned, gently, not to expect to be.
