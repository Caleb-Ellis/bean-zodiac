Braised Edamame are the Bean Zodiac's most hardworking — they understand work is required and follow through.

Edamame know what needs doing without dressing it up; Spicy Beans make the effort urgent and immediate; Boiled Beans sustain the operation with steady patience over time.

The Braised Edamame's challenge is assuming others share their ethic. They must learn to recognize different kinds of contribution and leave room for others' pace.
