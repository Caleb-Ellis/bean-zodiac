Braised Pinto Beans are the Bean Zodiac's moodiest — bright at noon, dark by evening, the pot never quite the same twice. They feel the grey coming two rooms off and name it before anyone else has shivered; the people around them are spared a lot of surprises by living with one.

The shadow is the message reread four times until the chile blooms, and the reply that lands hotter than the morning's version of them would have allowed. By breakfast they have moved on. The other person, sometimes, has not.
