Braised Pinto Beans are the Bean Zodiac's moodiest — their weather changes constantly and means it each time.

Pinto Beans' interior shifts with every angle of light, and so does their emotional weather; Spicy Beans turn each shift into an immediate, total event; Boiled Beans hold it all in a long, slow heat that doesn't release quickly.

The Braised Pinto Bean's challenge is transmission. They must learn to signal the weather that will matter to those nearby, not every shift.
