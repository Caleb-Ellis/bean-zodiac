Brined Black Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most methodical — they move through the world in sequence because it is how they think.

Black Beans observe before acting; Sour Beans cut away what doesn't belong; Boiled Beans let each stage complete at the speed it requires.

The Brined Black Bean's challenge is accepting that not everyone works the same way. They must learn to trust processes different from their own.
