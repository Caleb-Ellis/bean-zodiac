Navy Beans are principled and capable; Spicy Beans have been through things and emerged intact; Roasted Beans hold warmth turned mostly inward.

The Peri-Peri Navy Bean's challenge is receiving. They must notice when self-reliance has stopped being a virtue and become a default.
