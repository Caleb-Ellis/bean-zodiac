Desiccated Mung Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most clinical. In a real emergency they're the useful one: pressure on the bleeding, an address for the ambulance, panic postponed. Afterwards the same steadiness reads cool. Weeping alongside someone, no fix offered, isn't help they reach for.
