Miso Mung Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most poetic — when they speak, the phrase lands and stays.

Mung Beans see what is ordinary as vivid; Umami Beans turn observation into resonance; Fermented Beans work things in the dark until form emerges.

The Miso Mung Bean's challenge is plainness. They must learn to send the plain draft when that is what is needed.
