Aged Cannellini Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most distinguished — depth and refinement in combination, accumulated quietly over time.

Cannellini Beans improve every context through precision; Umami Beans reward patience with something irreplaceable; Dried Beans hold it all in a form that does not explain itself.

The Aged Cannellini Bean's challenge is the reputation that precedes them. Distinction earned over time can make it harder to be seen fresh. These Beans must occasionally let people encounter them without the context — and trust what they find.
