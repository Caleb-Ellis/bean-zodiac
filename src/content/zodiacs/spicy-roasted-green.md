Peri-Peri Green Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most optimistic — they show up certain it will work.

Green Beans' optimism is structural and produces evidence; Spicy Beans remove any hesitation about declaring this; Roasted Beans fill the room until others can feel it and act on it too.

The Peri-Peri Green Bean's challenge is when things genuinely don't work out. They must hold their positivity and reality in the same hand.
