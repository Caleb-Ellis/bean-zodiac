Peri-Peri Green Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most exuberant. At a friend's first gig they're at the front, singing along to words they learned that afternoon. Occasionally they've planned your celebration before asking whether you wanted one. Enjoying something quietly from a seat near the back is a thing they'd have to be taught.
