Glazed Fava Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most outspoken — they say the clear thing warmly and without hesitation.

Fava Beans know what they think and say it clearly; Sweet Beans deliver this as a generous offering; Roasted Beans carry it outward with warmth.

The Glazed Fava Bean's challenge is timing. They must find the moment when the truth lands rather than cuts.
