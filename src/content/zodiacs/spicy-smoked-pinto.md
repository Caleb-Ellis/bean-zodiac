Chipotle Pinto Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most erratic — the expression vivid, the temperature high, the direction gone before the eye can follow it.

Pinto Beans wear their complexity openly and stain whatever they touch; Spicy Beans make every shift feel sudden and total; Smoked Beans let the whole pattern drift from a source no one can reliably name.

The Chipotle Pinto Bean's challenge is pattern. Brilliance at high heat, untethered, eventually scorches everyone in the room. These Beans must choose one thread and walk it far enough to show where it was leading all along.
