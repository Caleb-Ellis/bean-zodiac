Rendered Navy Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most nostalgic. They get everyone from the old flat into one room with the same terrible playlist on, and it works. The plan can quietly become having all of it running again, exactly as it ran. Wanting to know what next year holds is a curiosity that has passed them by.
