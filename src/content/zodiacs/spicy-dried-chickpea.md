Sichuan Chickpeas are the Bean Zodiac's most conductive — they fit into every environment, and in every environment, eventually, something shifts.

Chickpeas adapt to any space; Spicy Beans make the energy immediate and hard to contain; Dried Beans concentrate it, so even small contact produces significant transfer.

The Sichuan Chickpea's challenge is the change they carried through without noticing. These Beans move freely and transmit energy wherever they go — not always intentionally. They must learn to notice what they are leaving behind them.
