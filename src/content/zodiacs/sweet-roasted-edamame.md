Glazed Edamame are the Bean Zodiac's most diligent — they do the work carefully and completely, warming every step with genuine effort.

Edamame count every step and make each count; Sweet Beans make the effort feel warm; Roasted Beans hold the heat steady toward completion.

The Glazed Edamame's challenge is recognizing when the work is good enough. They must learn to let it go.
