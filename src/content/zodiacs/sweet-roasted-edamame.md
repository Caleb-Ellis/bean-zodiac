Glazed Edamame are the Bean Zodiac's most considerate — they do the work with the people it serves in mind, warmly and without announcement.

Edamame do the work that needs doing; Sweet Beans give the doing warmth; Roasted Beans carry the warmth outward to whoever the work is for.

The Glazed Edamame's challenge is the care that crowds out the self. The one who tends everyone else also needs tending.
