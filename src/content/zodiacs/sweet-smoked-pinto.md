Barbecued Pinto Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most dreamlike — the story stays vivid in the air, the teller already half-remembered.

Pinto Beans wear their markings openly and season whatever they inhabit; Sweet Beans make those markings feel like an invitation; Smoked Beans set the whole display behind glass, near enough to read and too far to touch.

The Barbecued Pinto Bean's challenge is embodiment. Expression that outruns the body leaves the story orphaned; these Beans must learn to stand fully inside the thing they are giving away.
