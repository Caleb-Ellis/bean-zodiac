Cultured Kidney Beans ferment slow and dark. They give nothing up at the surface.

Kidney runs deep in the pot; bitter refuses anything unexamined; fermented works without fire, on its own clock. The three together make a paste that asks for years and then sits at the back of a spoon.

The danger is the seal held too long — pressure with no vent, a crock that cracks before it pours. Better the paste worked into broth where it can be tasted, not the jar opened only when something has already gone wrong.
