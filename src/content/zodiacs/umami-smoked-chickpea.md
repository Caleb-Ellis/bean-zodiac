Hickory Chickpeas are the Bean Zodiac's most humble. Fifteen years in the job, and they still ask the newest person how they'd do it. Occasionally they hand on the one piece of work that nobody else could have done. Saying out loud that they were the best in the room is a sentence they can't finish.
