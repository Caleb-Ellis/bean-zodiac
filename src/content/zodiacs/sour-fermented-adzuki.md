Pickled Adzuki Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most self-aware — they have examined themselves until the picture is unsentimental.

Adzuki Beans celebrate and avoid; Sour Beans cut through both; Fermented Beans examine until nothing remains unlooked-at.

The Pickled Adzuki Bean's challenge is not confusing self-knowledge with self-acceptance. They must learn to be in what they know without commentary.
