Hickory Kidney Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most seething — the passion has gone down to a temperature that is not visible from the surface but can be felt from across the room.

Kidney Beans colour everything they inhabit with an intensity they do not always name; Umami Beans turn that intensity inward and slow; Smoked Beans give the whole thing a quality that seems to come from the walls.

The Hickory Kidney Bean's challenge is expression. Seething without outlet becomes sediment. These Beans must find the release that matches the scale of what has been accumulating.
