Desiccated Kidney Beans are the Bean Zodiac's toughest — they have been through the thing that would flatten most people and came out denser for it, calm in the kind of bad week that sends everyone else into a spiral. They take the news standing up. Nothing surprises them anymore, and there's a real peace in that.

The shell that kept the bad out, though, keeps everything out. New affection arrives and finds no soft place to land; they catch themselves drafting the polite distance before they've even decided they want it, armour worn so long it's stopped feeling like a choice.
