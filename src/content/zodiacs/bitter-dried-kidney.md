Desiccated Kidney Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most durable. The kettle is twenty years old because they replaced the element, and the friendship thirty because they kept answering. A position taken at twenty-two can set so hard that nobody will raise it again. Buying the cheap one because it only has to last a year makes no sense to them.
