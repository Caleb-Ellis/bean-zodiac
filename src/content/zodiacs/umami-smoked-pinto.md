Hickory Pinto Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most atmospheric — their expressiveness has become weather: something you are inside of rather than observing.

Pinto Beans season the whole pot with what they carry; Umami Beans give that depth a weight that rewards sustained attention; Smoked Beans let the combination become the quality of the air itself.

The Hickory Pinto Bean's challenge is foreground. Atmosphere is the context for experience, not the experience itself. These Beans must step out of the background long enough to become the subject.
