Infused Chickpeas are the Bean Zodiac's most worldly. Two minutes into your story they name the part you haven't got to yet, having lived it twice. Occasionally somebody's big news gets met with a nod and the story of the last person it happened to. Being amazed on cue is a face they cannot manage.
