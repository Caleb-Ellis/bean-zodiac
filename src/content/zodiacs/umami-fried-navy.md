Tempura Navy Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most arrogant — they've developed real competence and stopped seeing its limits.

Navy Beans know what is required and do it; Umami Beans provide genuine depth; Fried Beans deliver at temperature without second-guessing.

The Tempura Navy Bean's challenge is wrongness. They must develop the habit of doubt before they need it.
