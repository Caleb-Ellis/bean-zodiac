Red-Hot Edamame are the Bean Zodiac's most bold — they go first without waiting for ideal conditions.

Edamame assess what is needed and find it sufficient; Spicy Beans make delay feel costly; Fried Beans move immediately, at full temperature.

The Red-Hot Edamame's challenge is knowing when the made moment is too soon. They must occasionally check whether they are going first or going alone.
