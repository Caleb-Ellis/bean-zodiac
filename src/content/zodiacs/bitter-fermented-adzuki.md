Cultured Adzuki Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most nostalgic — they hold to what has proved itself in the dark, and bring it forward by the spoonful.

Adzuki Bean knows which sweetness is worth keeping; Bitter Beans cure away the sentimental until only the salt-true remains; Fermented Beans work inward, slowly, where no one is watching.

The Cultured Adzuki Bean's challenge is knowing when the old crock feeds the table and when it only crowds the shelf. The past is a larder, not a hiding place. Open the jar to season the meal in front of you — not to avoid cooking it.
