Cultured Black Beans keep their work behind a lid. The brine darkens, the skins slacken, and nothing reads on the outside of the crock until the cook lifts the cloth.

Black bean carries its colour into the liquor only slowly; bitter strips the dish of anything that has not earned salt; fermentation refuses to be hurried, and ruins the batch if forced.

The crock must eventually be opened. A culture left past its hour goes from sour to acrid; the cook who never ladles becomes the cook who throws the jar out. Pour the mojo while the beans still know how to take it.
