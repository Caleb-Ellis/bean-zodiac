Barbecued Kidney Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most fond. Their car has a door that doesn't lock and they'll tell you what's good about it for as long as you'll stand there. Sometimes the café they've gone to for years becomes a café other people are only visiting. Being neutral about anything they like is not a state available to them.
