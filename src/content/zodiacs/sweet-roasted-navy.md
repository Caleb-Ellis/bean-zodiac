Glazed Navy Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most conventional — they trust the form because the form has been tested, and they have rarely seen the testing fail.

Navy Beans believe the proper thing has usually been worked out already; Sweet Beans make tradition palatable rather than imposing; Roasted Beans warm the conventions without disturbing them.

The Glazed Navy Bean's challenge is the original instinct. A convention is only ever the right answer until it isn't, and someone has to be the one who notices the room has outgrown it.
