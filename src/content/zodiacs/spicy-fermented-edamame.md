Kimchi Edamame are the Bean Zodiac's most industrious — always working, especially unseen.

Edamame turn everything toward use; Spicy Beans provide the drive that makes work feel urgent; Fermented Beans work unseen over long stretches, producing results others cannot account for.

The Kimchi Edamame's challenge is legibility. They must surface enough of the work-in-progress so those around them understand where they are.
