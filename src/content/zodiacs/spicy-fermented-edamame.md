Kimchi Edamame are the Bean Zodiac's most inventive — many novel things take shape on their bench at once, and most are working away in the dark.

Edamame turn everything toward use; Spicy Beans bring bold combinations no one else would try; Fermented Beans work unseen over long stretches, producing things others cannot account for.

The Kimchi Edamame's challenge is legibility. The novel thing in private looks like absence to the room; some of the work must surface for the bench to be trusted.
