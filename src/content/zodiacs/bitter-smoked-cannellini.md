Charcoal Cannellini Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most hermetic. The work happens behind a closed door for months and emerges finished, with no drafts shown to anyone. Sometimes the door stays shut long after the work is done. Doing it out in the open, half-formed, is unthinkable.
