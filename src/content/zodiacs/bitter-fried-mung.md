Scorched Mung Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most brisk. Tell them at eight that you can't face the day and by nine you're dressed, fed, and out of the house with them. Occasionally the help lands before you'd finished saying what was wrong. Talking somebody round slowly, one gentle mention at a time, is a pace they cannot hold.
