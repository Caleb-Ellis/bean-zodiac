Pajeon for the Scorched Mung Bean: a batter that wants resting, greens that wilt at a temperature half a degree off, a vinegar dip mixed and tasted before the oil is poured.

Mung soaked overnight and the chalk gone from its centre. Bitter greens trimmed, salted, pressed. The pan brought up slowly, the first drop of batter listened to before the ladle commits.

The dish rewards the cook who checks the crust at the rim before lifting. It also feeds the cook who doesn't — flipped early, eaten standing, the middle still loose around the spoon.
