Pickled Kidney Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most defensive — they know exactly what they are protecting and why.

Kidney Beans protect by nature; Sour Beans clarify what is worth protecting; Fermented Beans harden positions slowly and deeply.

The Pickled Kidney Bean's challenge is distinguishing protection from closure. They must ask whether what they're defending still needs defending.
