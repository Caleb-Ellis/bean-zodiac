Scorched Fava Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most fearless — they face what is difficult without hesitation.

Fava Bean decides work is worth doing; Bitter Beans assess and price the risk; Fried Beans act immediately.

The Scorched Fava Bean's challenge is that fearlessness can look like failing to take things seriously. They must occasionally name what they assessed and overcame so others see this isn't carelessness.
