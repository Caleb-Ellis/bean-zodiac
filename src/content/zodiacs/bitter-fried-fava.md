Scorched Fava Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most fearless — they meet hot oil without flinching and shape the patty in the same motion.

Fava decides the work is worth the doing; bitter weighs what the work will cost; fried commits the hand before the mind can stall it.

The Scorched Fava Bean's trouble is that a quick hand reads, from the doorway, as a careless one. They do well to speak the weighing aloud now and then, so the kitchen knows the leap was measured before it was taken.
