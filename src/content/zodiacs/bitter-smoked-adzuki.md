Charcoal Adzuki Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most oracular. They knew the relationship was over the night everyone else called it a rough patch, knew which colleague would quit before the colleague did, felt the room turn a sentence before it turned. They are right with a frequency that unsettles the people who love them.

The trouble is they keep the reading to themselves until it's safe to be proved correct. "I had a feeling," they say afterward, every time, and they did — but a warning delivered at the wake is just a tidier way of saying nothing. They'd rather be quietly vindicated than loudly wrong, and so the one person who saw it coming is also the one who let it.
