Miso Black Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most introspective — they live substantially inside.

Black Beans have interiors the surface never suggests; Umami Beans make the depth real and rewarding; Fermented Beans work on their own schedule at their own temperature.

The Miso Black Bean's challenge is return. They must maintain the practice of bringing things up from that depth so it remains a source rather than a retreat.
