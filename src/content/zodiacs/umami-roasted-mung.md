Rendered Mung Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most sympathetic. Four words into the bad news their face has already done the rest, and you tell them all of it. Sometimes the crying at your trouble is theirs, and you end up making the tea. Keeping a thing light so nobody has to feel it is a trick they were never shown.
