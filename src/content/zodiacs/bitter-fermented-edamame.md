A pod of cultured edamame opens onto a bean that has been thinking longer than the cook has.

Edamame keeps only what survives the brine. Bitter holds back the nod until the tongue can name what it's nodding to. Fermentation tests the premise the rest of the kitchen took on faith.

The danger is a clean argument served cold. A conclusion poured over silken tofu without warming the bowl first will slide off the spoon untasted. The pod is the case; the broth around it is what makes anyone willing to eat.
