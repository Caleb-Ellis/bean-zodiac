Dehydrated Chickpeas are the Bean Zodiac's most wry. They report the disaster with one eyebrow up and the whole table survives the evening better for it. Sometimes the eyebrow arrives at a genuine grief. Taking something entirely at face value, straight-faced, they can't manage.
