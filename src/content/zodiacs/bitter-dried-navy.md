Dried Navy Beans are the zodiac's surest hand at the inherited recipe. The crock is the crock their mother used; the molasses is measured against the same wooden spoon; the oven door stays shut for the hours it has always stayed shut.

Navy holds the form that was given. Bitter pares away anything that has not proved its keep. Dried refuses the easy give of water and time, and asks the cook to plan the day before.

The danger is the rule that has outlived its reason — salt pork in a pot that no longer needs it, a long bake for beans already tender. A method worth keeping can still bear a question at the threshold of the kitchen.
