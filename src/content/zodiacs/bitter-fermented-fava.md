Cultured Fava Beans sit at the long end of the Bean Zodiac, the ones who reach for a second spoon when others have set theirs down.

Fava Bean strips a thing twice before trusting it; Bitter Beans hold the tongue back from easy agreement; Fermented Beans do their work below the lid, out of sight, on their own clock.

The Cultured Fava Bean's hazard is the third tasting, and the fourth. A pot interrogated past its hour goes cold. Some brines are finished before the cook is.
