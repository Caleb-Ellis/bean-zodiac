Dashi Adzuki Beans are the Bean Zodiac's most perennial. They get back in touch after five years of nothing and pick up the exact conversation you were having. Sometimes the evening everybody quietly dreads goes in the calendar again, because it always has. Calling something finished for good is a verdict they never quite reach.
