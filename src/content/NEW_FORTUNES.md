# Fortune rewrite — direction & guidance

## The problem being solved

Across the 30 adzuki zodiacs (and by extension all 360), the fortunes share a single emotional register — quiet, knowing, slightly melancholic, full of kettles and doorways and unsent letters. It's a mood, and the mood is "literary fiction protagonist alone at dusk." For a site whose premise is **beans as zodiac**, that's weirdly somber. The bit is funny; the fortunes forgot.

Recurring tells across the set:

- "The room has been waiting" used 4× verbatim or near-verbatim.
- "You already know which" / "you already know" used 6×.
- Lamp / kettle / window / door / napkin as the dominant props.
- "Walk in [adverb]" / "Arrive [adverb]" as the default fortuneMost opener in the high-energy quadrant.
- Near-twin trait clusters: sincere / transparent / candid all writing the same "send the plain version" fortune.
- Everything in the same hushed tone, as though the umami beans wrote all 360.

## The direction

**Range is the product.** A sweet roasted chickpea should feel like a different narrator than an umami fermented black bean. Three axes shape the voice:

- **Flavour drives register** — the tonal _quality_ of the voice (dry, tart, propulsive, earnest, oracular).
- **Form drives rhythm and stance** — the _posture_ of the speaker (steady, austere, oblique, kinetic, expansive, hidden).
- **Bean drives worldview** — the _concerns_ and reference frame the fortune draws on (celebration, depth, calm, refinement, etc.).
- **Trait drives angle** — the specific _what_ of the advice within all of the above.

Think of it as: flavour is the _tone of voice_, form is the _body language_, bean is the _life the speaker has lived_, trait is the _topic on the table_.

### Flavour registers (tone)

- **Bitter** — dry, mordant, discerning. The friend who roasts you and is right. Cultivated, slightly cold.
- **Sour** — tart, clarifying, a little gleeful. Horoscope that's slightly enjoying your problem. Honest to the edge of nasty.
- **Spicy** — crackling, dare-shaped, propulsive. Kicks you out of the chair. Demands attention now.
- **Sweet** — generous, unembarrassed, comforting. The sincerity is the joke and also it's real. Risk: lazy/cloying.
- **Umami** — patient, deep, slightly oracular. Knows something it isn't saying. Rewards staying with the thing.

### Form registers (rhythm / stance)

- **Boiled** — steady, composed, sustaining. Long sentences allowed; the fortune holds the reader. Risk: melancholic drift.
- **Dried** — austere, concentrated, stripped. Short. No ornament. Built to outlast. Risk: brittle.
- **Fermented** — oblique, unconventional, internal. Says it sideways; the meaning surfaces an hour later. Risk: lost in itself.
- **Fried** — kinetic, decisive, fast. Verb-first. Doing is the thinking. Risk: irascible / scorched.
- **Roasted** — warm, expansive, convivial. Generous in its detail. Risk: hedonistic, slack.
- **Smoked** — enigmatic, elusive, uncanny. The source is offstage; you only feel the residue. Risk: vague mistaken for deep.

### Bean worldviews (concerns / frame of reference)

- **Adzuki** — celebration as serious practice. Knows what deserves marking; avoidant when nothing does.
- **Black** — depth and observation. Tests before trusting; opacity is the shadow.
- **Butter** — calm, ease, rest as a choice. Lowers the room's temperature; inertia is the shadow.
- **Cannellini** — refinement and editing. Removes the unnecessary; perfectionism is the shadow.
- **Chickpea** — connection, adaptability, travel. Fits any room; uncommitted is the shadow.
- **Edamame** — practicality, efficiency, the next move. Cuts preamble; dismissive is the shadow.
- **Fava** — courage, difficulty as worth-it. Moves toward the hard thing; reckless is the shadow.
- **Green** — urgency, freshness, momentum. Acts before ready; restless is the shadow.
- **Kidney** — passion, protection, full commitment. Stands between; overextended is the shadow.
- **Mung** — healing, gentle care, regeneration. Tends conditions; insecure / used-up is the shadow.
- **Navy** — principle, systems, dependability. Knows the right thing; rigidity is the shadow.
- **Pinto** — expression, creativity, story. Wears the interior outward; overemotional is the shadow.

### Reference fortunes (one per flavour, fortuneMid slot)

- **bitter-roasted-adzuki:** Reply to the text. You've drafted it nine times; the tenth will be worse. Send the seventh.
- **sour-boiled-adzuki:** A small betrayal at lunch — nothing dramatic, just someone ordering what you wanted. Petty is information.
- **spicy-fried-adzuki:** Do the loud version. The quiet version has been workshopped to death and it died.
- **sweet-fermented-adzuki:** Tell them you love them in the dumbest possible way — a meme, a sticker, a song link with no caption.
- **umami-smoked-adzuki:** Between 3 and 4pm, a stranger will hand you a small correct sentence. Write it down.

## Craft principles

1. **Add a verb of action.** Most current fortunes are observations. The best horoscopes recommend a tiny, slightly absurd ritual — _light a candle for the email you didn't send._ Give the user something to actually do with the fortune; that's half the fun.

2. **Concrete, not weirdly specific.** Named objects, small dares, oddly confident predictions are good. _Over_-specifying (hour windows, exact counts, named months) reads as a tic and gets old fast. The current fortunes are specific about _feelings_ but vague about _world_ — flip that, but don't overcorrect into trivia.

3. **Stakes and surprise.** A great fortune has a small twist — sets you up to expect one thing, delivers another. Don't just confirm what the trait already implied.

4. **End on the punch.** The last sentence is the line that lands; if there's a tail after it explaining the joke or naming the lesson ("Note who", "The form is the affection", "Pick up the phone instead of typing", "it was the one where you sounded like a person"), cut the tail. Trust the reader to get it. If you can delete the final clause and the fortune is sharper, the clause was the writer reassuring themselves.

5. **Bean-mentions are seasoning.** The bean premise can stay visible occasionally (`the adzuki in you wants to be left alone in hot water`) but sparingly — if every fortune name-checks beans it gets cutesy fast.

## Guardrails (learned the hard way)

1. **Keep the bite.** Voice-flavouring must sit on top of a sharp instruction, not soften it. If the rewrite is longer and more qualified than the original, it has failed.

2. **No vagueness.** Every fortune must contain a clear, concrete instruction or prediction. "Try the slower one, even if it's worse" is a hedge dressed as a voice choice — cut it. "Answer the version that survives them" sounds wise but means nothing — cut it. The original "Try the slower one" is the model.

3. **Voice is a flavour, not a costume.** Don't apply voice rules as mechanical devices (one parenthetical per fortune, one em-dash interrupt, etc.) — the device shows and reads as mannered. The voice should feel like a person who naturally talks this way, not a writer wearing a uniform.

4. **The cut is the verdict.** For voices that imply weighing or deliberation (judicious, philosophical), the weighing happens _before_ the fortune — the fortune itself is what survived. Don't weigh in the delivery.

5. **De-dupe the near-twin traits.** sincere / transparent / candid (and similar clusters) need genuinely distinct angles, not synonymous "send the plain version" fortunes. Sincere = warmth-forward honesty; transparent = nothing-hidden visibility; candid = bluntness as efficiency.

6. **Don't strip the instruction for the sake of voice.** Early rewrites cut concrete instructions ("walk in slower," "flag the receipt") and kept only the abstract image. The instruction is the bone; voice is the meat. Don't debone.

7. **Don't lean on "the room."** It's become a tic — "the room turns," "the room reads," "watch the room change." Once or twice across a flavour is fine; using it as the default stage for any social moment makes the whole set sound like one narrator. Reach for the specific place instead (the kitchen, the meeting, the table, the doorway, the thread) or cut the framing entirely.

8. **The trait is the speaker; flavour and form are accents.** Trait drives who's talking and what they care about. Flavour and form colour the voice but must not override the trait. A nostalgic bitter-fermented bean is still nostalgic *first* — the bitterness is the edge that keeps it from going saccharine; the fermentation is a slight obliqueness in delivery. If the rewrite reads as "a bitter narrator commenting on nostalgia" instead of "a nostalgic narrator with a dry edge," the registers are playing too loud.

## Working method

- Don't read each file — grep the fortune fields, then rewrite via a script that does in-place field replacement (`/tmp/rewrite_fortunes.py`).
- Do one trait at a time so the user can pump the brakes if a voice drifts.
- Draft the voice rule (one short line) and the five fortunes together, in chat, before applying.
- When drafting, check against the flavour register first — does this _sound_ sour/spicy/sweet/bitter/umami? — then check the trait angle within it.
