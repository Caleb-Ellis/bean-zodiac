Fermentation requires no fire, no outside force. It is transformation from within, in conditions no one else sees. The result is something the original ingredient could never have predicted: complex, alive in a way that fresh things are not, arrived at under its own power.

Fermented Beans do their most important work out of sight — processing, converting, arriving at conclusions that others cannot follow. They are not quick to share what they're working on.

The Fermented Bean's shadow is strangeness for its own sake — the risk of going somewhere no one else can follow. Fermentation and spoilage use the same mechanism. The Fermented Season asks you not to get lost within yourself.
